`Aeration`'s `alpha`, and with `temperature_correction=True` its `kla_theta`
correction, now apply to a kLa set by a DO controller (`do_setpoint=`) or a
control signal (`kla_signal=`), exactly as to a fixed `kla`: the controller
output is the clean-water kLa, and a fouled tank asks its controller for more
of it. Gases stripped with oxygen follow the corrected kLa. Results with the
defaults (`alpha=1.0`, no temperature correction) are unchanged. The aeration
energy and blower air flow of `evaluate_bsm1` / `evaluate_bsm2`, and
`AerationUnit.kla_vector` / `oxygen_kla`, now report the clean-water kLa for a
fixed `kla` too, so a tank with `alpha` below 1 no longer reports less aeration
energy than a clean one; pass `process_water=True` to `kla_vector` /
`oxygen_kla` for the kLa times `alpha`.
