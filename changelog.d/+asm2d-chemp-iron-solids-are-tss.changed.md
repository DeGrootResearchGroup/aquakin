In `asm2d_chemp`, the iron precipitates (strengite and ferrihydrite) now add
their molar mass to the suspended solids `XTSS`, so the Takács settler and the
TSS readings count them; before, they weighed nothing. A ferric-dosed plant on
this model settles slightly more solids. The model also has a composition
table now (the strengite carries its phosphorus), so its mass balance,
conservation check and composition-based measurements work.
