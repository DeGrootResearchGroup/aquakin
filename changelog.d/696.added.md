`aquakin-ui` now edits a chemical dosing unit's reagent in its form: choose the
model the reagent is written in, give it a label, and list the species it
contains with their concentrations (each species chosen from that model, with
its unit shown). A dosing unit is no longer marked "Python" in the palette. A
sequencing batch reactor still is: its phases and settling model can only be
set from Python.
